State Street CDO dashboard — 500 simulated transaction rows
Open cdo-dashboard/index.html after extracting the archive. Keep the dashboard files together.
The six simulated-tables CSVs preserve the supplied column headers. The browser loads the
equivalent records from cdo-dashboard/data.js. Editing a CSV alone does not change the dashboard.

Simulation covers July 6–10, 2026, with USD valuation and Eastern timestamps.
There are 490 distinct transaction IDs and ten repeated feed rows within the 500 rows.
There is one pipeline event per feed row, linked by transaction ID. The CSV preserves feed
order, so both rows of a duplicate ID can be paired by their row positions.
Positions aggregate valid, deduplicated trade quantities by account, security, and trade day.
They represent daily traded exposure for the simulation, without an opening balance.
The position CSV has no settled quantity or currency column. The dashboard uses the position
quantity as its simulated settled quantity and the security master currency for valuation.
For cutoff eligibility, 425 of 475 eligible events meet the deadline. Twenty-five events have no cutoff.
The four additional supplied tables are outside the current dashboard inputs.
